# Digital Systems & Experiments

I'm curious about how the digital world works, and how its different parts fit together.
From operating systems and networks to programming, cloud infrastructure, hardware and open-source software, there are plenty of things I want to understand better.
I use this space to collect notes, try things out, and document what I learn along the way.

## Things I'm Exploring

- **Systems & Infrastructure** : operating systems, networking, cloud and security.
- **Software & Programming** : different languages, web technologies and automation.
- **Open Source & Alternatives** : independent tools, alternative platforms and different ways to solve problems.
- **Hardware & Creative Technology** : computer systems, AI and unconventional digital projects.

## Learning in Progress

I'm still building my foundations, exploring different approaches and figuring out which tools are right for each problem.
This repository will grow along with that process, one experiment at a time.

## A Note on This Space

Some projects may be small experiments or work in progress; others may become more complete projects. I'll try to document what I was exploring, what worked, and what I learned.
I may also write some notes in Italian when it feels more natural to explain an idea that way.
